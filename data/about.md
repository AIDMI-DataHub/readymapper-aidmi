# ReadyMapper — 2026 Nepal Floods

ReadyMapper is a rapid disaster-impact map for response teams. It pulls satellite
damage assessments, observed flood extent, population movement, and on-the-ground
news reports into one view, so responders can see where impact is concentrated and
prioritise assessment and relief.

This deployment covers the August 2026 Rasuwa & Nuwakot flood in Nepal, prepared by
AIDMI with CrisisReady.
